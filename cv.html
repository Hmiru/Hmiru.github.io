01 · Research Interests

I build player-conditioned football world models that generate on-ball events and off-ball trajectories in one framework. I want player representations that hold steady when the team, the manager and the match state around a player change.

04 · Research Experience

CIDA Lab, University of Seoul · Graduate Research Assistant 2025–present Computational Intelligence and Data Analytics Laboratory · Advisor: Prof. Sang-Ki Ko
  • Built ScoutGPT, an autoregressive Transformer that treats a football match as language. The model splits each event into discrete attributes: actor, location, and action type. It predicts the next attribute from the previous ones, the lineup, and the match context.
  • Trained the model on 6.1M K League events from 2,283 matches between 2021 and 2025. Added a value-prediction head aligned to VAEP. The model performs better than CatBoost, LSTM, and sequence-model baselines on next-event prediction and goal prediction.
  • Added counterfactual transfer simulation with Monte Carlo sampling. The method puts a player into a new lineup and measures the change in offensive progression and goal probability. It reduces post-transfer VAEP forecast error against a naive carry-over baseline.
  • Current work extends the framework to tracking data for trajectory generation and player embeddings that transfer across clubs. Role-masking makes sure that positional role does not become a shortcut in cross-season player retrieval.
Pixelscope · Industry Collaboration 2026, 3 months
  • Built a pipeline that derives structured event data from raw player tracking data, reaching F1 > 0.90 on most kick events and F1 > 0.85 on ball control.
